# Sahidica NT 4.1.0 rights memorandum

**Status:** provisional documentation; clarification recommended before describing rights as fully resolved
**Checked:** 2026-08-18
**Project use:** free public electronic comparative edition of the Four Gospels

This memorandum records Urevangelium's current rights basis. It is project documentation, not legal advice.

## Material used

Urevangelium pins Sahidica NT 4.1.0 as distributed through Coptic SCRIPTORIUM. The site reproduces its normalized Gospel word-groups with edition and occurrence provenance.

## Direct permission statement

The current Coptic SCRIPTORIUM licensing page reproduces Sahidica's statement that the edition may be used free of charge in free electronic editions of the New Testament when the full title and copyright information are credited:

- https://copticscriptorium.org/download/corpora/Mark/coptic_nt_sahidic.html
- https://ebible.org/find/details.php?id=copshc

Urevangelium is free to readers and presents an electronic New Testament textual-history edition, so public display appears to fit the expressly stated category, subject to full attribution and any applicable edition terms.

## Documentation discrepancy

Coptic SCRIPTORIUM documentation has also characterized Sahidica-derived material with “academic use only” language. That description is not perfectly congruent with the free-electronic-edition permission above:

- https://copticscriptorium.org/download/final-performance-NEH-pres-access-foundations-2016.pdf

The project must not silently choose whichever wording is broader. The direct licensing page materially strengthens the basis for public display, but the project should still preserve the exact 4.1.0 distribution notice and seek clarification from Coptic SCRIPTORIUM covering repository storage and redistribution of pinned files or derived word-group data. The current public contact is `contact@copticscriptorium.org`.

## Current safeguards

- Pin the precise source version and retain source hashes.
- Provide the full Sahidica title, version, credits, and required copyright notice.
- Keep the project free to readers.
- Do not imply that SCRIPTORIUM endorses Urevangelium.
- Distinguish source reproduction from Urevangelium alignment and auditing.
- Do not transfer permissions for Sahidica automatically to Horner, Logos, KELLIA, or other datasets.

## Open actions

1. Archive the exact Sahidica 4.1.0 license/distribution notice used at acquisition.
2. Identify the appropriate rights contact.
3. Request written clarification for Urevangelium's public display and repository use.
4. Record the response, date, correspondent, scope, and permission identifier.
5. Recheck the public attribution against the notice before deployment.

Until those actions are complete, the manifest should describe the rights basis accurately rather than claiming an unqualified open-data license.
